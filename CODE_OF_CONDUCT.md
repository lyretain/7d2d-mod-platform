# Hordepin Community Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Examples of positive behavior include using inclusive language, respecting different experience levels, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, threats, sexualized content, publishing another person's private information, deliberate disruption, or any conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository Issues, pull requests, Discussions, project-operated services, and public spaces where someone is representing Hordepin.

## Enforcement

For repository content, use GitHub's report or block functions and, when safe, notify the maintainers without reposting harmful or private material. Reports will be reviewed confidentially to the extent supported by GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants.

Enforcement decisions should consider impact, context, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

## Attribution

This policy is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), adapted to the current maintainer and reporting channels of this project.
